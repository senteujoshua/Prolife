Footcare

Diabetes footcare

Proper foot care is essential for people with diabetes to help prevent complications. We offer specialised diabetic footwear and orthotic solutions that provide pressure relief, enhance circulation and reduce the risk of ulcers and infections.

Children and adults

Clubfoot management

We provide effective clubfoot management solutions, including braces and corrective orthotic devices, designed to support proper foot alignment and improve mobility in children and adults with congenital clubfoot.
